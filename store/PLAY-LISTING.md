# Google Play listing text for Kames

**App name:** Kames
**Short description (80 characters max):** Six calm games: chess, Ludo, Bagh-chal, Chungi, Keshey, Tic-Tac-Toe.

**Full description:**
Kames is a small collection of games from Nepal, Bhutan and around the world, in one calm app.

- Bagh-chal: tigers and goats, the board game from Nepal
- Chess: full rules, two players or against the computer
- Chungi: keep the rubber-band ball in the air, solo or with up to four players
- Keshey: Bhutanese wrestling. Hold the waist, keep your balance, throw your opponent
- Ludo: 2 to 4 players, with or without the computer
- Tic-Tac-Toe: two players or against the computer

No ads. No account. No internet permission. Your scores stay on your phone.

**Category:** Games > Board (or Casual)
**Privacy:** the app collects no data and has no permissions. Google Play still asks for a privacy policy link: a one-line page saying so is enough.
